Power & batteries

Most beginner problems are power problems. Low voltage only — never mains.

SourceVoltageGood forNotes
USB from computer / phone charger5 VLearning, desk projectsEasiest and safest
3 × AA (alkaline)~4.5 VPortable gadgetsInto VSYS on Pico; fine for days
4 × AA NiMH~4.8 VRechargeableGood for servos
Single LiPo / Li-ion 186503.7 VWearables, small robotsUse a protected cell + proper charger board (TP4056 with protection)
USB power bank5 VOutdoor projectsSome switch off at low current — add a small load or pick an "always on" bank

Rules of thumb

  • Common ground: every part must share GND, or readings go haywire.
  • Motors and servos get their own supply (shared GND) — they cause resets if they share the board's.
  • 3.3 V boards (Pico, ESP32): never feed 5 V into a GPIO pin.
  • Estimate battery life: capacity (mAh) ÷ average current (mA) = hours. A Pico W with Wi-Fi ≈ 50 mA → 2000 mAh lasts ~40 h. Use deep sleep to stretch it to weeks.

Battery safety

  • Never puncture, bend or short a lithium cell. Don't charge it unattended or on a bed.
  • Swollen battery? Stop using it, keep it outside on a non-flammable surface, take it to battery recycling (B-cycle drop-off points across Australia).
  • Kids: use AA packs, not loose lithium cells.
