Components cheat sheet

What the common parts in a starter kit do, how to wire them, and the mistakes everyone makes once.

Buses in one line each

  • I²C — two wires (SDA, SCL) shared by many sensors, each with an address.
  • SPI — four wires, faster; good for screens and SD cards.
  • UART / Serial — TX → RX and RX → TX; how your board talks to the app over USB.
  • PWM — fast on/off to dim LEDs, set servo angles and play tones.
  • ADC — reads a voltage as a number (0–4095 on ESP32, 0–65535 in MicroPython on Pico).

Starter kit shopping list (around A$40–70 at Jaycar, Core Electronics or Altronics)

  • Raspberry Pi Pico W or ESP32 dev board, USB cable that carries data
  • Half-size breadboard, jumper wires (male–male, male–female)
  • LEDs, 220 Ω and 10 kΩ resistors, 2 buttons, a potentiometer, a piezo
  • One sensor you're excited about (BME280, light, distance)
