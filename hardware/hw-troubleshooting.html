Troubleshooting

Work through these in order — 9 times out of 10 it's one of the first three.

  1. Is it plugged in properly? Many USB cables are charge-only. Try a different cable.
  2. Is the right port picked? In the app's Connect button, choose the board (often "USB Serial" or "Pico").
  3. Is GND shared? Every part's GND must connect to the board's GND.
  4. Right pin number? The GP number (GP15) is not the physical pin number (pin 20). Check the pinout.
  5. LED backwards? Long leg to the + side.
  6. Breadboard gap: the middle channel splits rows; the power rails on some boards are split halfway.
  7. Nothing on serial? Match the baud rate (115200 is common) and make sure the code prints with a newline.
  8. Board keeps resetting? A motor or too many LEDs — give them their own power.
  9. Web Serial missing? Use Chrome or Edge on desktop/Android. iPhone Safari doesn't support Web Serial or Web Bluetooth yet.
  10. Hot part? Burning smell? Unplug straight away. Check for a short between 3V3/5V and GND.

Ask ULTRA well

Paste: the board, what you wired (pin → part), your code, and the exact error. Ask "What are the 3 most likely causes, most likely first?"
