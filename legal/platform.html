Archangel platform overview

A practical guide to how Archangel fits together. Updated 28 October 2026.

What it is

Archangel is a free creative studio that runs in your browser (and installs like an app). You describe what you want, and it helps you build it — apps, games, books, notes — and keeps it on your device. Cloud and AI are add-ons you choose.

The four parts

  • Builder — turns an idea into a working single-file app. Pick a blueprint or describe your own, answer a few questions, and it writes the app. You can preview, restyle (Looks), version and export it.
  • ULTRA — the helper that runs through everything. It answers questions, plans, writes and takes actions in the studio. It works on-device by default; you can switch to Gemini, Grok or the shared AI.
  • Community — optional. Sign in to share apps, comment, like and remix. Built on Supabase, moderated by admins.
  • Marketplace — where apps and helpers are listed. Free now; paid sales with a 5% platform fee come later (Stripe).

Builder blueprint (how an app gets made)

 1. Idea / blueprint  →  2. Questions  →  3. Spec (screens, data, features)
        →  4. Generate app (HTML)  →  5. Preview & test  →  6. Look / edit / version
        →  7. Own it (record + licence)  →  8. Export / share / list

Design principles

  • Free first. Everything everyday is free.
  • Local first. Works offline; your data is yours.
  • Plain language. No jargon where a normal word will do.
  • You own it. Export anything, any time.
  • No keys in code. AI keys live server-side or in your own settings.

Tech stack

React + TypeScript + Vite, a PWA service worker, Supabase (optional cloud), Cloudflare Workers (AI proxy), WebLLM (on-device AI), hosted on GitHub Pages.
