Seller terms

Plain-English template — not legal advice. Needs review by an Australian lawyer before real money goes live. Payments are not switched on yet — sales are recorded as commitments only.

All rights reserved: Archangel Vault AI / Michael Glenn James Manning.

When you list

  1. You confirm you made the item (or own the rights to it) and that it isn't a prohibited item.
  2. You describe it honestly, including if AI helped make it.
  3. You pick a licence. The buyer gets exactly that licence — nothing more.
  4. You pick a mode: Free, Give away, Buy now (fixed price), Make an offer or Auction.

Prices, fees and payouts

  • Prices are in Australian dollars (AUD).
  • Listing is free. When a sale completes, Archangel keeps the house fee (currently 5%) and the card processor (Stripe) charges its own card fee. Your listing shows the breakdown.
  • When payments are switched on, money will be paid to your own Stripe account (Stripe Connect). Archangel will never hold your bank details.
  • You are responsible for your own tax (see Australian tax guide).

After a sale

  • Deliver the item (for digital items, the app gives the buyer access) and honour your licence.
  • You can't change the price, terms or end time once an auction has bids, or mark items as sold yourself — Archangel's server records sales.
  • You can remove an active listing at any time before it has bids or a buyer.

Things we may do

Hide or remove listings that break the guidelines, cancel orders that look fraudulent, and ban accounts that break the rules.
