Australian tax guide for creators

General information only — not tax, financial or legal advice. Check the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) at https://www.ato.gov.au or ask a registered tax agent about your situation.

All rights reserved: Archangel Vault AI / Michael Glenn James Manning.

Hobby or business?

  • Occasional sales of things you make for fun may be a hobby, and hobby income is generally not taxable.
  • If you sell regularly, aim to make a profit, or run it like a business, it's likely a business and the income is assessable. See the ATO's "Hobby or business" guidance.

GST

  • You must register for GST if your business turnover is $75,000 or more a year (check the ATO for the current threshold).
  • If you're registered, you may need to charge GST (10%) on your sales and lodge a BAS.

ABN

If you're carrying on a business you can apply for an ABN (free) at https://abr.gov.au.

Record keeping

Keep records of sales, fees and costs for 5 years. Your fee and earnings statement in each paperwork pack, and your order history, can help.

Income tax

Business income goes in your tax return. You may be able to claim costs (for example, equipment or software) — check with the ATO or a tax agent.
