Builder guide — blueprints, looks & themes

Blueprints

Open Builder. With nothing open you'll see the blueprint gallery. Tap one to start, or "Describe your own". Apps: Habit tracker, Journal & mood, Budget & bills, Workout log, Shop / catalogue, Study & flashcards, Events & RSVP, Recipe book, Pet care. Games: Arcade game, Quiz game, Memory match.

Blueprint tab

Every app gets a Blueprint tab: a schematic of its screens, data and features. Tap Save SVG to keep a copy.

App looks

In the Blueprint tab, pick a Look to restyle your app in one tap — Clean, Celestial, Midnight, Neon, Ember, Mint, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Warm, Rose gold, Lavender, Candy, Bright, Elegant, Mono, Retro. Each look saves a new version, so you can always go back.

Studio themes

Settings → Appearance changes how Archangel itself looks: Prismatic, Nebula, Aurora, Solar, Daylight, Seraph, Celestial, Crimson, Ocean, Rose, Mint, Midnight, Ember.
