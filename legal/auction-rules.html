Auction rules

Plain-English template — not legal advice. Needs review by an Australian lawyer before real money goes live. Bids are commitments only — no card is taken and no money moves until payments are switched on.

All rights reserved: Archangel Vault AI / Michael Glenn James Manning.

  1. Start price — the first bid must be at least this.
  2. Minimum increment — every later bid must beat the current bid by at least this amount.
  3. Reserve (optional, hidden amount) — if bidding doesn't reach it, the item doesn't sell. Listings only say whether the reserve has been met.
  4. Length — auctions run from 1 hour to 30 days.
  5. Anti-sniping — a bid in the last 2 minutes extends the end to 2 minutes after that bid, so everyone gets a fair chance to respond.
  6. No self-bidding — sellers can't bid on their own items; banned accounts can't bid.
  7. Outbid notices — you get an in-app notice when someone outbids you.
  8. Winning — when the clock runs out, the highest bid at or above the reserve wins. The winner and seller are notified and an order is created (Awaiting payment).
  9. Bids can't be withdrawn once placed, except where the law requires it or an admin cancels a fraudulent auction.
  10. All bid checks happen on Archangel's server, not in your browser, so they can't be faked.
