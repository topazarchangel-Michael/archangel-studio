Archangel roadmap

Updated 28 October 2026. Plans, not promises — order and dates can change.

Archangel stays free for everyday users. Paid parts (Marketplace sales, paid testers) are optional.

✅ Shipped

  • Studio core — projects, uploads, notes, tasks & calendar, missions, streaks, themes, works offline (PWA).
  • ULTRA helper — on-device helper, optional Gemini / Grok / shared AI, chat that can take actions in the app.
  • Builder — describe an app or game, answer a few questions, get a working app you own.
  • Builder upgrade (Phase 1) — 12 blueprints (habit tracker, budget, recipes, arcade, quiz, memory match…), the Blueprint tab with a schematic you can save as SVG, 17 one-tap app looks, 8 new studio themes.
  • Community — optional sign-in, share apps, comments, likes, admin tools and bans.
  • Marketplace preview — free listings, showcase of the 5% fee model.
  • Ownership & docs — authorship records with SHA-256 fingerprints, licences, terms & privacy templates.
  • Backups & sync — JSON backup, cloud backup, device-to-device sync.
  • Legal & docs (Phase 2) — privacy policy, terms, licences & credits, this roadmap, platform overview, About & Legal page.
  • AI resources (Phase 3) — free-first guide to which AI to use (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, on-device, shared AI, Grok), and step-by-step guides to building, testing, adding AI to your apps, and teaching/training AI. In Settings → AI resources and on the web.
  • Hardware guides (Phase 4) — safety first, which board (Pico, ESP32, Arduino), first circuits with schematics, connecting devices to your apps (Web Serial, Web Bluetooth, Wi-Fi), schematics & blueprints, and device + app ideas the Builder can start. In Settings → Hardware guides and on the web.

🔜 Still to come from Phase 3

  • Better on-device models; smarter picking between local and cloud.
  • More prompts, tutorials and starter kits in the resource library.

🔧 Still to come from Phase 4

  • More device projects and sensor walkthroughs.
  • Guides for running Archangel and local AI on your own gear — old laptops, mini PCs, home servers.

🧪 Phase 5: Paid testers

  • Opt-in tester programme: early builds, feedback rewards, bug bounties.

💳 Phase 6: Stripe & paid Marketplace

  • Real payments through Stripe. Sellers keep 95%; Archangel takes 5% (plus Stripe's fee).
  • Seller onboarding, payouts, refunds, receipts, GST notes.

🌅 Later

  • Android app, team projects, publishing apps to their own web address, more blueprints and looks.
