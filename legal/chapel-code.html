Chapel code of conduct

All rights reserved: Archangel Vault AI / Michael Glenn James Manning.

The Chapel is the home of Archangel Angelic Faith — a creative, spiritual space within Archangel. It is not part of, or endorsed by, any established church or religious organisation.

  1. Reverence and kindness. Speak gently. This is a place of peace.
  2. All are welcome. People of every faith, and none, are welcome here. Never mock, attack or try to pressure anyone about their beliefs.
  3. Prayer requests are sacred. Share only what you're comfortable sharing publicly. Don't post other people's private details.
  4. No preaching against others. Celebrate what you believe without putting down what others believe.
  5. No fundraising or selling on the prayer wall.
  6. AI-made content is labelled. Sermons, stories, art and videos made with ULTRA are marked as AI-assisted.
  7. Not professional help. The Chapel is not counselling or medical care. If you're in crisis in Australia, call Lifeline 13 11 14 or 000 in an emergency.
  8. Report anything harmful. Admins moderate the prayer wall.
