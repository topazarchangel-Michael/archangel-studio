Privacy Policy — Archangel

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Plain-English note: this is a template and general information, not legal advice. If you have a serious question, talk to a qualified lawyer.

The short version

  • Archangel is free and works on your device by default. What you make stays in your browser.
  • Nothing goes to the cloud unless you turn on a cloud feature (Community, cloud backup) or an AI provider.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

What's stored on your device

  • Your projects, apps, notes, tasks, settings and themes — in your browser's local storage.
  • Uploaded files (images, PDFs, docs) — in your browser's IndexedDB.
  • Offline copies of the app itself — in the service worker cache, so it opens without internet.
  • None of this leaves your device on its own. You can export a backup or delete everything from Settings.

Optional cloud features (only if you switch them on)

  • Community & cloud backups use Supabase (a hosted database). If you sign in, we store your email, display name, the posts, comments and apps you choose to share, and any backups you upload.
  • Shared apps and posts are public to other Archangel users — don't share anything private.
  • Admins can hide posts or ban accounts that break the Terms.
  • You can delete your posts, and ask us to delete your account (see Contact).

Optional AI providers

  • The built-in helper works on your device (local rules, or a small model that downloads and runs in your browser).
  • If you choose Google Gemini, xAI Grok, or the shared Archangel AI, your message and the context you attach is sent to that provider to get an answer. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • The shared AI runs through a small proxy (Cloudflare Workers) that passes your request on. We don't keep a log of your chats there beyond basic rate-limit counts.
  • Don't paste passwords, card numbers or other sensitive info into AI chats.
  • API keys are never built into the app's code.

Device-to-device sync

  • Sync devices connects your own devices directly (peer-to-peer via PeerJS). A public signalling server helps them find each other; your data travels device to device.

Cookies & storage

  • We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Browser storage is used only to make the app work (see above).
  • If you sign in to cloud features, Supabase stores a sign-in token in your browser.

Kids & games

  • Archangel includes simple games and kid-friendly templates, but the app isn't aimed at collecting info from children.
  • Under 13s should only use cloud features (Community, sign-in) with a parent or guardian. Local, offline use needs no account.
  • Games you build that other people play: if they collect any info, add your own privacy note (there's a template in Ownership & docs).

Where data lives

  • Local data: on your device. Cloud data: Supabase and Cloudflare servers, which may be outside Australia.

Your rights

  • You can see, export and delete your local data any time (Settings → Backup / Delete).
  • For cloud data, contact us to access, fix or delete it. Australians can also contact the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

  • If this changes, we'll update the date above and mention it in Settings → Updates.

Contact

  • Open Feedback inside the app, or contact the project owner via the GitHub page: https://github.com/topazarchangel-Michael
