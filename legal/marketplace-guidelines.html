Marketplace & community guidelines

Plain-English template — not legal advice. These terms must be reviewed by an Australian lawyer before real money is switched on. Today, payments are not switched on: no money moves through Archangel.

All rights reserved: Archangel Vault AI / Michael Glenn James Manning.

What you can share

  • Things you made in Archangel: apps, games, music, music videos, poems, art and images, books and templates.
  • Things you have the right to share. If AI helped you make it, say so in the description.

Listing is free

  • Posting to the Gallery, listing on the Marketplace and giving things away are always free.
  • A house fee (currently 5%) applies only to a completed sale or a won auction. See Fees.

How to behave

  • Be kind. Feedback, ratings and comments should be about the work, not the person.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, scams, spam, fake reviews or rating manipulation.
  • No bidding on your own items (shill bidding), fake offers, or bids you don't mean to honour.
  • Respect other people's faiths and beliefs everywhere, including the Chapel.

Safety and moderation

  • Tap Report on anything that breaks these rules. Three reports hide an item until an admin checks it.
  • Admins can hide, restore or delete posts, comments and listings, and ban accounts. Banned accounts can't post, react, bid, offer or buy.
  • See Prohibited items for what can't be listed at all.

Your rights

You keep ownership of what you make. You choose a licence when you list (see your paperwork pack). Buyers only get the rights that licence gives them.
