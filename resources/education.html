Education — learn and teach with Archangel

Build learning apps for your kids, your class or yourself, and use ULTRA as a patient tutor.

Ways to learn here

  • Teach page — pick a topic and ULTRA makes a short lesson, worked examples and a quiz. Ask it to "explain like I'm 10" or "give me harder questions".
  • Notes — paste class notes and ask ULTRA to make flashcards or a summary.
  • Books — read and ask questions about the book you're reading.

What makes a learning app work (the research in short)

  1. Retrieval practice — testing yourself beats re-reading.
  2. Spacing — review just before you'd forget (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month).
  3. Interleaving — mix question types rather than 20 of the same.
  4. Feedback with a reason — always show *why* an answer is right.
  5. Small wins — progress bars, streaks and badges keep people coming back. Don't punish missed days too hard.

Prompts that work well

  • "Make a 10-card flashcard deck on the water cycle for Year 5, with one picture idea per card."
  • "Build a mini course teaching budgeting to teenagers: 5 lessons, a quiz after each, and a certificate."
  • "Quiz me on Australian states and capitals, getting harder when I'm right."

For parents and teachers

  • Apps you build are private by default and work offline.
  • Check facts ULTRA writes — AI can be wrong. Use the 🧪 Test it guide before sharing with a class.
  • Keep student names and photos out of shared apps.
