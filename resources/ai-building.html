Build an app or game with AI — step by step

Beginner-friendly. Works with any brain in Settings; cloud brains give richer results.

1. Pick a brain (2 minutes)

  1. Open Settings → ULTRA's brain.
  2. Leave Built-in if you just want to try, or get a free Gemini key for better results (see Which AI?).
  3. Tap Test to check it answers.

2. Describe what you want

  1. Open Builder.
  2. Pick a blueprint (habit tracker, budget, quiz, arcade…) or type your own idea.
  3. Be specific: who it's for, the 3 main things it does, and the look. Example: "A chore chart for two kids, with stars, a weekly reset and a bright, friendly look."
  4. Answer ULTRA's questions — short answers are fine.

3. Check the plan before building

  • Open the Blueprint tab: the schematic shows screens and how they connect.
  • Change anything that's wrong now — it's cheaper than fixing it later.

4. Build, then improve in small steps

  • Build, then try the app in the preview.
  • Ask for one change at a time: "Make the buttons bigger", then "Add a reset button".
  • If something breaks, say what you did and what happened. ULTRA can roll back.

5. Own it and share it

  • Settings → Export gives you the full source with a README for GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.
  • Your Ownership & docs record stamps when you made it.

Prompt tips that work

  • Say the goal, not just the feature.
  • Give an example of the data ("Monday: dishes — Ella").
  • Ask for plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript if you want to edit it yourself.
  • Short and clear beats long and vague.
