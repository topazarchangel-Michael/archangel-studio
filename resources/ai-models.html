Which AI should ULTRA use?

Updated 28 October 2026. Guidance only — not endorsements. Prices, limits and model names change often; check the provider's page before you rely on them.

Start free. Archangel never needs a paid AI. Every option below can be switched in Settings → ULTRA's brain, and you can change your mind any time.

The options in detail

1. Built-in helper (always there)

  • Good for: quick actions — add a task, take a note, open a page, start a Builder blueprint.
  • Limits: not a real chatbot. Simple wording works best.
  • Privacy: nothing leaves your device.

2. Shared Archangel AI

  • Good for: proper chat and Builder help without making an account anywhere.
  • Limits: shared by everyone, so there are fair-use limits and it may be busy at peak times. It may be off in some test builds.
  • Privacy: your message goes through Archangel's small proxy to the AI provider. Don't send very private things.

3. Google Gemini (free key — recommended free upgrade)

  • Good for: writing app specs, explaining code, long documents, building apps and games in the Builder.
  • Strengths: generous free tier, large context (it can read a lot at once), good at code.
  • Limits: per-minute and per-day limits on the free tier. On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products.
  • Get a key: aistudio.google.com/apikey — no card needed.

4. Groq (free key)

  • Good for: fast back-and-forth chat, quick fixes, short code edits.
  • Strengths: very quick; runs open models like gpt-oss.
  • Limits: daily limits on the free plan; smaller context than Gemini.

5. OpenRouter free models

  • Good for: trying different open models without paying.
  • Limits: it picks a free model each time, so answers vary. Some free models may log prompts.

6. On-device AI (Qwen, in your browser)

  • Good for: private notes, offline use, simple questions.
  • Strengths: free forever, works without internet once downloaded, nothing leaves the device.
  • Limits: needs WebGPU (recent Chrome / Edge, many Android phones) and 1–3 GB of graphics memory. Smaller and slower than cloud AI — not great for big code.
  • Tip: start with Small — recommended. Download on Wi-Fi.

7. xAI Grok (your own key, paid)

  • Good for: strong reasoning and code, if you already have xAI credits. grok-build-0.1 is tuned for code; grok-4.3 is the cheaper all-rounder.
  • Limits: paid per use and billed by xAI, not Archangel. Only use it if you want to.

When to use which (for building)

  • Planning an app or game: Gemini or Shared AI. Ask for a short spec first.
  • Writing the code: the Builder does this for you. A cloud brain (Gemini, Groq, Grok) gives richer apps than on-device.
  • Fixing a bug: Groq for speed, Gemini if you need to paste a lot of code.
  • Private or offline work: On-device.
  • Hit a limit? Switch to another free option in Settings — your projects don't change.

Keys stay yours

  • Paste keys only in Settings → ULTRA's brain. They stay in this browser — not in backups, sync, feedback, exports or source code.
  • Never paste a key into chat, a note, an app you build, or GitHub. If you do, delete it at the provider and make a new one.
  • Archangel never charges for AI. Any paid AI is between you and that provider.
