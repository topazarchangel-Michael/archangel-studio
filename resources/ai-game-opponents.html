Challenging AI opponents for games

A great game AI is beatable but never dumb. Aim for players winning about half the time.

Pick the right brain for the game

Game typeTechniqueNotes
Noughts & crosses, Connect Four, draughtsMinimax + alpha-betaSearch a few moves ahead; deeper = harder
Card / dice gamesExpected value + simple rulesNever peek at hidden cards
Chase / shooter / racingState machine (wander → chase → flank → retreat) + predictionAim where the player *will* be
Strategy / cityUtility AI (score every option, pick best)Easy to tune with weights
Puzzle hintsSame search, used for the playerOne AI, two jobs

Adaptive difficulty (the secret sauce)

  1. Keep a level 1–10, saved on the device.
  2. After each game: player wins → level + 1; AI wins → level − 1; draw → stay.
  3. At low levels, the AI sometimes plays a random legal move: chance = 0.55 − level × 0.055 (level 10 never blunders).
  4. Board games: search depth = 1 + level ÷ 2. Action games: enemy speed, reaction delay and aim error scale with level.
  5. Show the level so players feel progress. Offer Reset and a fixed Practice mode.

Make it feel smart, and fair

  • Explain moves: "I blocked your column", "I'm setting up a double threat".
  • Personalities: Cautious (defends), Balanced, Aggressive (attacks) — just different scoring weights.
  • No cheating: the AI only knows what a player in its seat would know.
  • Think time: wait 300–800 ms before moving so it feels human.
  • Hint button: run the same AI for the player's side.

Blueprints ready in the Builder (Game tag)

  • ⭕ Noughts & crosses vs AI — perfect minimax at level 10, adapts below that.
  • 🔴 Connect Four vs AI — alpha-beta, depth 1–7, centre-column scoring.
  • 👾 Arena chase vs smart AI — enemies predict and flank you; waves get smarter.

Test your game AI

  • Play 10 games at each level: does level 1 lose often and level 10 almost never?
  • Let the AI play itself 100 times — no crashes, no illegal moves, games always end.
  • Time the AI's move on an older phone: under 1 second.
  • Ask ULTRA: "Play the AI's best move in this position and explain why." Compare with what the game did.
