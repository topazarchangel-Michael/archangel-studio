Teaching and "training" AI

What training really means, and the free, practical ways to make AI better at your thing.

Most people don't need to train a model

Training a model from scratch costs a fortune. Instead, you can teach an existing model with good instructions and examples. That gets you most of the benefit for free.

Level 1 — Teach ULTRA (free, in Archangel)

  • Open Teach ULTRA and add facts, preferences and how you like things done.
  • Fill in Your profile so answers fit you.
  • It stays on your device.

Level 2 — Good instructions (free)

  • Write a short "house style" note: language, look, file layout, what to avoid.
  • Paste it at the start of a Builder request, or keep it in Notes and ask ULTRA to follow it.
  • Include 2–3 examples of what good looks like.

Level 3 — Your own knowledge (free)

  • Put guides, rules or product info in Notes or My Files. ULTRA can search them and answer from them — this is called retrieval (or RAG).

Level 4 — Fine-tuning (advanced, optional)

  • Fine-tuning adjusts an open model with hundreds of your own examples.
  • Free or cheap ways to learn: Google Colab free notebooks, Hugging Face courses and models, Unsloth guides for small models.
  • Start with a small model (1–3B). Keep a test set of examples it never trained on, so you can check it actually improved.
  • Only use data you own or have permission to use. Never train on other people's private information.

Free places to learn

  • Hugging Face Learn — courses on models and fine-tuning.
  • Google AI Studio — try prompts and Gemini models free.
  • fast.ai — free practical deep-learning course.
  • Each AI provider's own docs for limits and pricing.
